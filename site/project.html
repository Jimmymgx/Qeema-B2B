The Jazarti app is designed to transform the fresh meat shopping experience, making it easier and more convenient than ever before. The project idea was to provide a digital platform that enables customers to browse different types of meat and select the cuts that precisely meet their needs, with the option to place orders and receive them on the same day.

The goal was not just to sell products online but to offer a comprehensive experience that gives customers confidence in product quality, ease of ordering, and fast delivery, along with secure payment methods and real-time order tracking services.

Main Focus / Mission Statement

  1. 1

    The Challenge

    The biggest challenge we faced was developing an app that clearly displays meat products to help customers choose the right cuts, while providing a fast ordering experience that includes secure payment, quick delivery, and continuous order tracking.

  2. 2

    Implementation Mechanism

    The Qima Tech team is developing a specialised e-commerce application for selling meat, with products organised into clear categories, providing secure electronic ordering and payment methods, in addition to a comprehensive system for managing orders and tracking delivery operations.

  3. 3

    Customer journey with us

    The project management collaborated with the Qima Tech team to transition the traditional meat purchasing experience to a modern digital platform. By studying market needs and customer requirements, an application was developed that facilitates easy ordering and enhances the efficiency of sales and delivery operations.
